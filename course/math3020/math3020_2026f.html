Grade Calculation

In iCollege, there are 20 points for each of the 8 quizzes, and 100 points for each of the 3 tests. After lowest 2 quizzes and 1 test dropped, one can get 120 points for quizzes and 200 points for tests. The final exam is in-person and has 100 points.

Example. Suppose a student gets

  • 105 of 120 points in quizzes after dropping the lowest two,
  • 175 of 200 points in tests after dropping the lowest one,
  • 80 of 100 points in the in-person final.

Then this student gets a total score (105/120) * 30% + (175/200) * 40% + (80/100) * 30% = 85.25% and will be assigned a grade of B (83%-86%).
